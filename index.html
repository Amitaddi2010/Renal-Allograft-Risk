About this model

The calculator uses the 11 strongest predictors of the study's ElasticNet model (donor age and source, sibling donor, induction, HLA-A/B/DRB1/DQB1 antigen mismatches and their total, and the T- and B-cell flow crossmatch shifts). Recipient age, sex and crossmatch dual-positivity are held at cohort averages, so patients far from the average on those may differ from the full model. Model developed on 443 PGIMER transplant recipients (83 biopsy-proven rejections), out-of-fold AUC 0.693, Hosmer-Lemeshow p = 0.51. Research use only; not a substitute for clinical judgement.

What these are. Our own implementations of three published methods, computed in this browser from IPD-IMGT/HLA protein alignments and published amino-acid constants: amino-acid mismatch in the style of HLA-EMMA (Kramer et al., HLA 2020) — donor residues the recipient carries on neither allele, with the solvent-accessible subset shown separately; electrostatic and hydropathy mismatch in the style of the Kosmoliaptsis scores; and HLA evolutionary divergence (HED), the Grantham distance between a person's own two alleles across the peptide-binding domain (Pierini & Lenz 2018). Solvent accessibility is computed with Shrake–Rupley on a representative structure per locus (HLA molecule only, peptide and co-receptors removed) and normalised by the Tien et al. 2013 maxima; a position counts as accessible at ≥ 25% relative ASA.

What these are not. They are not the output of HLA-EMMA, PIRCHE-II or the Kosmoliaptsis tools, and the values are not interchangeable with them — use them for ranking within this cohort, not as a substitute for those services. PIRCHE-II is deliberately absent: it needs a licensed peptide–MHC binding predictor, so it cannot be reproduced here honestly. HED describes one person's own genotype, not the donor–recipient relationship, and must not be read as a mismatch count.

Send this pair to PIRCHE-II, HLA-EMMA or IEDB external tools

PIRCHE-II and HLA-EMMA are closed tools with no open-source implementation, so this app does not attempt to reproduce their scores. Instead it prepares the input in the format each expects and gives you somewhere to paste the answer back, so their published numbers sit beside our own measures for the same pair.

                        Method, reference database and result ID
                        

                            

                            

                            
A donor eplet counts as mismatched when it is absent from every recipient molecule of the same class (class I: A/B/C together; class II: DRB1/3/4/5, DQB1, DPB1 β chains together and DQA1/DPA1 α chains together), which is the HLAMatchmaker intra- and interlocus comparison. Loads are counts of unique mismatched eplets. Immunogenic load counts mismatched eplets whose names appear in IE.xlsx; antibody-verified load uses the workbook's own flags. Antigen-level mismatches (first field) are the counts used in the thesis registers and the risk model.
