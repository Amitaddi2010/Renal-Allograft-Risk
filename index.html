01 // MULTIMODAL ALLORECOGNITION AXES

Three Complementary Biological Domains

Traditional broad HLA matching provides modest risk discrimination in contemporary immunosuppression. This doctoral pipeline captures covert alloreactivity across three distinct mechanisms.

Flow Cytometric Crossmatch

Sub-CDC cellular crossmatching detects low-level donor-reactive alloantibodies binding peripheral T and B lymphocytes. Provides an essential 82.1% Specificity and 79.6% Negative Predictive Value rule-out safety gate.

B-MCS & T-MCS Channel Shifts

Non-HLA Checkpoint SNPs

Confirms *PD-L1* rs4143815 as an authentic non-HLA genetic risk locus (Allelic OR: 1.38, Recessive OR: 2.49, p = 0.00037). Polymorphisms disrupt microRNA binding, impairing peripheral T-cell co-inhibition.

Molecular Eplet Repertoire
