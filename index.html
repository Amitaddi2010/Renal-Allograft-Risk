Three Complementary Biological Domains

Traditional broad HLA matching provides modest risk discrimination in contemporary immunosuppression. This doctoral pipeline captures covert alloreactivity across three distinct mechanisms.

Flow Cytometric Crossmatch

Sub-CDC cellular crossmatching detects low-level donor-reactive alloantibodies binding peripheral T and B lymphocytes. Provides an essential 82.1% Specificity and 79.6% Negative Predictive Value rule-out safety gate.

Tailored Luminex SAB Surveillance Protocol
  • Pre-Transplant Gate: Negative CDC crossmatch and flow crossmatch thresholding (< 20 MCS T-cell, < 80 MCS B-cell).
  • Month 3 Screening: Luminex single-antigen bead (SAB) assay targeted specifically to identify anti-163LG or anti-130Q dnDSA.
  • Month 6 Protocol: Protocol allograft biopsy if eplet burden exceeds institutional surveillance threshold and any de novo DSA is detected.
  • Month 12 Horizon: Longitudinal annual SAB panel and chronic allograft injury screening.
